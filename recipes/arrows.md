# AD&D Arrow & Bolt Recipes

Crafting arrows and bolts requires the **Bowyer/Fletcher** proficiency (or **Weaponsmithing** for specialty materials).  
All recipes assume a standard fletching bench, a knife, and a small forge or fire unless noted otherwise.

---

## Arrows

### Flight Arrow
*A light arrow designed for maximum range.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 12 arrows |
| **Crafting Time** | 2 hours |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher (no penalty) |
| **Range Modifier** | +20% to all range categories |
| **Damage** | 1d6 (S/M), 1d4 (L) |

**Materials (per 12):**
- 12 × straight, dried softwood shafts (e.g., pine, cedar), ¼" diameter, 28"–30" long
- 36 × goose or turkey feathers (3 per arrow, for fletching)
- 12 × small iron bodkin-style tips (light gauge)
- Fine linen thread or sinew (for binding)
- Beeswax (for waterproofing thread)

**Notes:** Shafts must be kiln-dried or air-dried for at least 1 month. Green wood will warp. These arrows sacrifice stopping power for distance.

### Cold Iron Arrow
*Forged from iron never touched by fire — cold-worked metal that disrupts fey magic.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 6 arrows |
| **Crafting Time** | 8 hours |
| **Proficiency** | Weaponsmithing (no penalty); extremely difficult — −4 penalty without specialty |
| **Special** | Counts as magical for purposes of hitting fey, demons, and iron-vulnerable creatures |
| **Damage** | 1d6 (S/M), 1d4 (L) |

**Materials (per 6):**
- 6 × hardwood shafts, 30" long
- 18 × feathers
- 1 × small piece of raw meteoric iron or naturally occurring bog iron
- Cold-working tools: hammer, anvil, files, drawplate
- Sinew binding

**Notes:** Cold iron is **never heated**. All shaping is done by hammering, grinding, and filing alone. This process is extremely time-consuming. The DM may rule that a dedicated cold-iron smith is required.

### Fire Arrow
*An arrow with a pitch-soaked cloth bundle near the tip, designed to set fires.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 12 arrows |
| **Crafting Time** | 1 hour (plus drying time) |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher (no penalty) |
| **Damage** | 1d6 piercing + 1d4 fire (on a hit); 5% chance to set flammable materials alight |
| **Range** | −1 range category (short range only at normal penalties) |

**Materials (per 12):**
- 12 × hardwood shafts with iron tips (standard arrows)
- 12 × strips of tightly wound linen cloth (~2" wide, 4" long)
- 1 flask of pitch or lamp oil
- Fine wire or copper wire (to secure cloth to shaft)
- Tinder box (to light before firing)

**Notes:** The cloth must be soaked in pitch at least 1 hour before use and allowed to set (not dry). Fire arrows must be lit immediately before firing. In wet weather, there is a 30% chance the cloth fails to stay lit. Cannot be stored loosely in a quiver — use a sealed case.

### Poison Arrow
*An arrow whose tip or shaft is coated in toxin.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 12 arrows |
| **Crafting Time** | 30 minutes (application only) |
| **Proficiency** | Herbalism or Poisoner's craft; mishandling = Save vs. Poison |
| **Special** | Effect depends on poison used (see poison tables) |
| **Damage** | 1d6 (S/M), 1d4 (L) + poison effect on hit |

**Materials (per 12):**
- 12 × standard arrows (any type)
- 1 dose of chosen poison (contact or injury type):
  - *Type A (Weak):* Save vs. Poison at +2; 10 hp damage on fail
  - *Type B (Moderate):* Save vs. Poison; 25 hp damage on fail
  - *Type C (Strong):* Save vs. Poison at −2; 35 hp / death on fail
  - *Type D (Lethal):* Save vs. Poison at −4; death on fail
- Small brush or cloth swab
- Gloves (leather or thick cloth)

**Notes:** Poison degrades: arrows should be used within 24 hours of application or effectiveness is halved. Rainwater and submersion destroy coating immediately. Note that use of poison is considered dishonorable or evil by most AD&D alignments and deities.

*A masterwork arrow tipped with mithril — lightweight, hard, and slightly magical.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 6 arrows |

- Sinew or silk binding

**Notes:** Mithril is extraordinarily difficult to work. Dwarven or Elven smiths are typically required. The resulting arrows are prized by rangers and elven archers. Each arrow is worth approximately 50 gp.

### Whistling Arrow
*A hollow-tipped arrow that emits a shrill whistle in flight — used for signaling.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 6 arrows |
| **Crafting Time** | 3 hours |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher (no penalty); Tinker/Craft helps |
| **Special** | Audible up to 600 ft in open air; can serve as a signal; may frighten animals (morale check) |
| **Damage** | 1d4 (S/M), 1d3 (L) — less effective due to hollow tip |

**Materials (per 6):**
- 6 × hardwood shafts, 30" long
- 18 × feathers
- 6 × hollow bone or thin-walled copper/bronze tubes (~1" long, drilled with tone holes)
- Small drill or awl (to carve tone holes)
- Sinew binding

**Notes:** The whistle head must be tuned — different hole arrangements produce different tones, which can be used as coded signals between scouts. Three distinct tones require three separate hole patterns.

### Grappling Arrow
*A heavy arrow fitted with a small grapple and trailing rope.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 1 arrow |
| **Crafting Time** | 2 hours |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher + Blacksmithing |
| **Range** | Short range only (heavy); max ~60 ft with a longbow |
| **Special** | On a hit against a surface, hooks in (Str check DC 14 to dislodge); supports up to 250 lbs |
| **Damage** | 1d4 (blunt/bludgeoning from weight) |

**Materials (per 1):**
- 1 × thick hardwood shaft, 32"–36" long (reinforced)
- 1 × 3-pronged iron grapple hook (~4 oz)
- 50 ft of silk rope or hemp rope (coiled and attached to shaft collar)
- Iron collar/ring at shaft base to secure rope
- Sinew or wire binding

**Notes:** The rope must be coiled carefully at the archer's feet before firing. Silk rope is preferred (lighter, less drag). A failed shot against stone or wood may require a Strength check to retrieve the grapple. Heavily penalized in wind.

## Crossbow Bolts

### Crossbow Bolt (Standard)
*A short, stout bolt for a light crossbow.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 12 bolts |
| **Crafting Time** | 2 hours |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher (no penalty) |
| **Damage** | 1d6 (S/M), 1d4+1 (L) — light crossbow |

**Materials (per 12):**
- 12 × straight hardwood shafts (oak or ash), ½" diameter, 12"–14" long
- 24 × stiff feathers or thin leather vanes (2 per bolt)
- 12 × iron quarrel tips (square cross-section)
- Sinew or waxed thread
- Beeswax

**Notes:** Crossbow bolts (quarrels) are shorter and stouter than arrows. The vanes are often made from leather rather than feathers. Keep dry — moisture warps the vanes.

### Incendiary Bolt
*A crossbow bolt bearing a small sealed clay or ceramic vial of alchemist's fire.*

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Yield** | 6 bolts |
| **Crafting Time** | 3 hours (plus 24 hours for alchemist's fire acquisition) |
| **Proficiency** | Bowyer/Fletcher + Alchemy (or purchase fire vials) |
| **Damage** | 1d4+1 piercing + 1d6 fire on impact; 1d4 fire next round unless extinguished |

**Notes:** Incendiary bolts must be carried in a padded, sealed quiver. They cannot be used underwater. Against structures, treat as a flask of oil + torch. Highly effective against trolls and fire-vulnerable creatures.

## General Crafting Notes

**Proficiency Checks:** A failed proficiency check results in wasted materials (roll 1d6: on a 1–2, all materials are lost; on 3–6, half materials are recovered).

**Tools Required (Basic):** Fletching jig, sharp knife, small saw, sinew/thread, beeswax, and a smooth flat surface for straightening shafts.

**Tools Required (Advanced):** Small forge, anvil, hammer, tongs, files, whetstone, crucible.

**Storage:** Standard arrows and bolts should be kept dry and away from direct sunlight. Poison-coated arrows must be stored separately, wrapped, and used within 24 hours. Fire and incendiary projectiles require sealed, padded containers.
